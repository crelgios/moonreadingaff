Why the Moon Matters in Astrology

The moon has always carried emotional meaning in human culture. It affects how people mark time, think about cycles, and describe moods. In astrology, the moon is often associated with instinct, memory, vulnerability, comfort, and the parts of a person that operate before logic has time to explain them.

That is why moon-based astrology feels different from sun-sign astrology. The sun sign is often connected with identity, expression, energy, and the way a person shines outward. The moon is more private. It describes what a person may need when no one is watching, how they respond emotionally, and what helps them feel safe.

This difference is exactly why Moon Reading can feel meaningful.
